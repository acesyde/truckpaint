## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Workshop trucks in My Game
The My Game tab SHALL list, after the official vehicles and under a **Workshop** heading, the trucks of the player's subscribed Steam Workshop mods (see the workshop-vehicles capability), each with its name, brand and the name of its mod. They SHALL have the same checkbox, status (Not generated, Generated, Game updated), filters, search and the same **Generate (n)** run as the official vehicles, with Select All and Select None counting them. A Workshop truck's status SHALL be that of the package `mod.<game>.<workshop id>.<truck id>`. The Kind filter SHALL hide the Workshop section when it is set to Trailers. The scan of the Workshop SHALL run with the scan of the game, off the user interface thread, and repeat with Refresh. When the Workshop folder cannot be found, the section SHALL say so in one line, and when it holds no truck mod, say that none was found. A mod that cannot be read SHALL NOT stop the scan: it is named in one line of the section with the reason. The tab SHALL say that a Workshop truck's content belongs to its author and is for the player's own use. A mod that is updated by Steam after its truck was made is regenerated by checking the truck and pressing Generate.

#### Scenario: Truck of a Workshop mod listed
- **WHEN** a subscribed mod of category `truck` defines a truck the game does not
- **THEN** the My Game tab lists it under Workshop with its mod's name and the status Not generated

#### Scenario: Generate a Workshop truck
- **WHEN** the player checks a Workshop truck and presses Generate (1)
- **THEN** its package is installed, the truck shows Generated with its version, and it is available in New Project

#### Scenario: Official and Workshop trucks in one run
- **WHEN** the player checks two official vehicles and a Workshop truck and presses Generate (3)
- **THEN** all three are made one after another, with the progress, cancel and failure rules of Generating packages

#### Scenario: Mod unreadable
- **WHEN** one mod's package is damaged
- **THEN** the other mods' trucks are listed and the damaged mod is named in the Workshop section with the reason

#### Scenario: No Workshop folder
- **WHEN** the Workshop folder is not found
- **THEN** the official vehicles are listed as before and the Workshop section says the Workshop could not be found

#### Scenario: Trailers filter
- **WHEN** the player sets the Kind filter to Trailers
- **THEN** the Workshop section is not shown

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Generated packages stay local
Generated packages SHALL be written only to the Vehicle Library on the player's computer. The application SHALL send nothing to any server while scanning or generating. Each generated manifest SHALL carry the license "Personal modding only, not for sale. Game content belongs to SCS Software.", except the manifest of a Workshop truck, which carries the license of the workshop-vehicles capability, and the My Game tab SHALL say that the game's content belongs to SCS Software, that the packages are for the player's own use, and that TruckPaint is not endorsed by SCS.

#### Scenario: License and notice
- **WHEN** a truck is generated and its details are opened in the Installed tab
- **THEN** its license is the one above, and the My Game tab shows the notice

#### Scenario: License of a Workshop truck
- **WHEN** a Workshop truck is generated and its details are opened in the Installed tab
- **THEN** its license names the mod's author instead of SCS Software

#### Scenario: No traffic
- **WHEN** the player scans and generates with no network
- **THEN** everything works
