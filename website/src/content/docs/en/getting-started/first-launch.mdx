The first time you open TruckPaint, it asks what it needs in three short steps, then lands you in your first project. After that, when no project is open, it shows its home screen.

## The first launch

You can leave at any time with **<Ui id="first-run-skip" />** (or Escape): TruckPaint keeps what you already chose and shows the home screen. It does not ask again.

### Step 1: language and game

<Shot name="getting-started/first-launch" alt="The first step of the first launch: the language, the games played, and for ETS2 the game folder found and the mod folder" />

- Under <Ui id="first-run-language" />, TruckPaint starts with your system's language. Choose another one to switch at once and keep it.
- Under <Ui id="first-run-games" />, check the game or games you play, at least one.
- For each game, TruckPaint looks for the installation in your Steam libraries and shows <Ui id="first-run-game-found" /> with its path, or <Ui id="first-run-game-not-found" />. Click <Ui id="first-run-choose-folder" /> to pick the folder yourself. A game that is not found does not stop you.
- For each game, it also shows the mod folder, the one <Ui id="cmd-export-mod" /> uses. Choose another one if your mods live elsewhere.

Click <Ui id="first-run-next" />.

### Step 2: vehicles to paint

<Shot name="getting-started/first-launch-vehicles" alt="The second step of the first launch: the trucks and trailers of the game, two of them checked" />

TruckPaint reads your game, without changing it, and lists the trucks and trailers you own. Search by brand or model, filter with <Ui id="vehicles-kind-all" />, <Ui id="vehicles-kind-trucks" /> and <Ui id="vehicles-kind-trailers" />, check the ones you want to paint, or use <Ui id="game-select-all" />.

Click <Ui id="first-run-generate" />. TruckPaint makes the checked vehicles on your computer, one after another, with a progress bar and <Ui id="button-cancel" />. A cancel keeps the vehicles already made, and a vehicle that fails is named without stopping the others. With nothing checked, the button reads <Ui id="first-run-continue" /> and you make vehicles later, in the <Ui id="vehicles-tab-game" /> tab of the Vehicle Library (see [Official vehicles](../../vehicles/official-vehicles/)).

If the game is not found, or if it is American Truck Simulator (not supported yet), the step offers <Ui id="vehicles-install-sample" /> instead. You can add another vehicle later with <Ui id="custom-open" />.

### Step 3: your first project

TruckPaint opens New Project on the game you chose first, with your new vehicles in the list. Continue with [Create your first project](../first-project/).

### Run it again

Choose <Ui id="menu-help" /> › <Ui id="cmd-run-first-launch" /> to go through the steps again, with your current choices already selected. From a project, TruckPaint first closes it and asks whether to save. Everything you chose can also be changed in the preferences, under <Ui id="prefs-games" />.

## The home screen

The home screen is where you start a new livery, open one you saved, or pick up recent work. It holds:

- **<Ui id="home-new-project" />**, the main button, which starts a new project (Ctrl+N, ⌘ on macOS);
- **<Ui id="cmd-open-project" />**, which opens a `.truckpaint` file you saved (Ctrl+O);
- **<Ui id="home-recent" />**, one card per project you opened, the most recent first;
- **<Ui id="home-preferences" />**, at the top right.

<Shot name="getting-started/home" alt="The TruckPaint home screen, with the New Project and Open Project buttons and a recent project card" />

The first time, the recent projects area is empty and offers <Ui id="home-recent-empty-create" />, which does the same as <Ui id="home-new-project" />. To create your first project now, go to [Create your first project](../first-project/).

## Reopen recent work

Each recent project card shows a thumbnail of the project's first main texture, its name, where its file is and when you last opened it. Click a card to open the project.

When a project's file was moved or deleted, its card is marked as unavailable and can't be opened. Its remove button (<Ui id="home-remove-recent" /> on hover) takes it off the list.

:::note[After a crash]
While a project has unsaved changes, TruckPaint keeps a recovery copy of it every two minutes, without touching your own file. If the app closes unexpectedly, the next launch shows <Ui id="home-recovered" /> above the recent projects. Choose <Ui id="home-restore" /> to get your work back, or <Ui id="home-discard" /> to throw the copy away.
:::

## Choose your language

TruckPaint speaks English, French, Spanish and German. At first it follows your system's language, and falls back to English for any other language.

To pick another one:

1. Open the preferences: <Ui id="home-preferences" /> on the home screen, <Ui id="menu-edit" /> › <Ui id="cmd-preferences" /> on Windows and Linux, or <Ui id="menu-app" /> › <Ui id="app-menu-settings" /> on macOS (Ctrl+,).
2. Under <Ui id="prefs-language" />, choose a language. The whole interface switches at once, without a restart.

Choose <Ui id="language-system" /> to follow your system's language again.

## Adjust the interface

The preferences also hold:

- <Ui id="prefs-ui-scale" /> and <Ui id="prefs-text-size" />, to make the interface and its text larger or smaller, with a live preview. <Ui id="prefs-reset" /> brings them back to 100%.
- On Windows and Linux, <Ui id="prefs-system-title-bar" />.

TruckPaint remembers your preferences, the size and place of its window, and your recent projects between sessions.
