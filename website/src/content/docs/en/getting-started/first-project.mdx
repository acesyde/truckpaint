In TruckPaint, a project is the paint job of a fleet: one or more trucks and trailers of **one game**, ETS2 or ATS, exported together as one mod. Every project starts from a vehicle, because a livery has to fit a vehicle's textures: there is no blank project.

This page uses the **TruckPaint Sample Truck**, which comes with the app and needs no game files. It works the same with any other vehicle.

<Clip name="getting-started/create-project" label="From the home screen, New Project opens, the sample truck is checked in the list, and Create Project opens the new project" />

## Create your first project

1. On the home screen, click <Ui id="home-new-project" /> (Ctrl+N, ⌘ on macOS).
2. Under <Ui id="vehicles-game-filter" />, keep **ETS2**: the sample vehicles are made for it. The list only shows the vehicles of the game you choose.
3. If no vehicle is installed yet, click <Ui id="vehicles-install-sample" /> under the list. It installs the sample truck and the sample trailer, without any download.
4. Click the **TruckPaint Sample Truck** row to check it. Its card opens on the right, under <Ui id="new-project-fleet" />.
5. Check what you want to paint on the card:
   - under <Ui id="new-project-cabins" />, one chip per cabin layout of the truck. The first one is checked; click another to paint it too;
   - under <Ui id="vehicles-accessories" />, the accessories, all checked at first.

   <Ui id="new-project-created" /> lists every texture the project will get, with its size.
6. Type a <Ui id="new-project-name" />, or leave it empty to name the project after the vehicle.
7. Click <Ui id="new-project-create" />.

<Shot name="getting-started/new-project" alt="The New Project dialog with the sample truck checked in the vehicle list and its card open under Your fleet" />

You can check several vehicles at once, trucks and trailers, and you can add more later: nothing you choose here is final. See [Trucks and trailers](../../fleet/trucks-and-trailers/).

:::tip[Not the vehicle you want?]
The list shows only installed packages. To get an official ETS2 truck or trailer, generate its package: see [Official vehicles](../../vehicles/official-vehicles/). For a vehicle without a package, use <Ui id="custom-open" />: see [Custom vehicles](../../vehicles/custom-vehicles/).
:::

## Look around

The project opens in the <Ui id="cmd-space-project" /> space: your fleet on the left, with one card per vehicle and a row per texture, and the information of the mod on the right.

<Shot name="getting-started/first-project" alt="The Project space of the new project, with the sample truck's card listing its textures, all Empty" />

Every texture starts **Empty**. Click one to open it in the <Ui id="cmd-space-workshop" />, where you paint on it over the vehicle's template. The top bar switches between the three spaces at any time: <Ui id="cmd-space-project" /> (Ctrl+1), <Ui id="cmd-space-workshop" /> (Ctrl+2) and <Ui id="cmd-space-brand" /> (Ctrl+3). The game of the project is shown next to its name, and can't be changed.

## Save your project

Choose <Ui id="menu-file" /> › <Ui id="cmd-save" /> (Ctrl+S). The first time, TruckPaint asks where to save, and proposes the project's name. A project is one `.truckpaint` file that holds everything, templates included: you can open it on another computer even if its vehicles aren't installed there.

The status bar shows <Ui id="status-saved" /> or <Ui id="status-unsaved" />. If you close the project or quit with unsaved changes, TruckPaint asks whether to save them first.
