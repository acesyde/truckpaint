TruckPaint paints vehicles it knows through **vehicle packages**. A package (a `.tpv` file) describes one truck or trailer: the templates of its textures, and the game data a mod needs, such as the vehicle's path in the game, the internal names of its cabins and the ids of its accessories. Every package has a version, so a vehicle can follow the game's updates.

Your vehicles come from four places:

- **official ETS2 truck and trailer packages** that you make from your own game, in the <Ui id="vehicles-tab-game" /> tab: see [Official vehicles](../official-vehicles/);
- the **sample vehicles** that come with TruckPaint, a truck and a trailer drawn for it, to try the app without game files;
- **custom vehicles** you make yourself from template files: see [Custom vehicles](../custom-vehicles/);
- `.tpv` files someone shared with you.

## Open the Vehicle Library

Choose <Ui id="menu-vehicle" /> › <Ui id="cmd-vehicle-library" />. It works from the home screen too, with no project open.

<Shot name="vehicles/library" alt="The Vehicle Library dialog, with the list of installed vehicles on the left and the details of the chosen vehicle on the right" />

The dialog has three tabs: <Ui id="vehicles-tab-installed" args={{ count: 2 }} />, with every installed vehicle, <Ui id="vehicles-tab-mine" args={{ count: 0 }} />, with only your custom vehicles, and <Ui id="vehicles-tab-game" />, where you make the official vehicles of your game. The first two show how many vehicles they hold. Above the list, search by name or brand, and filter by game and by kind.

Each row shows the vehicle's name, brand, kind and game, its newest installed version, and a status:

- <Ui id="vehicles-status-custom" />: a vehicle you made with <Ui id="custom-open" />;
- <Ui id="vehicles-status-update" />: the open project uses an older version of this vehicle than the newest one installed;
- <Ui id="vehicles-status-current" />: everything else.

## Read a vehicle's details

Click a vehicle to see its details on the right: a preview of its template, its name, brand, kind and game, the game versions it supports, and its paint job (the names of its main textures and its number of accessories).

Under <Ui id="vehicles-installed-versions" />, every installed version of the vehicle is listed. Several versions can be installed side by side: a project keeps the version it was made with until you update it.

## Install a package

Click <Ui id="vehicles-install" /> and choose one or more `.tpv` files. You can also drop `.tpv` files on the home screen or on an open project. A confirmation names each vehicle and version installed. Installing a version that is already there replaces it.

With no vehicle installed, the library explains what packages are and offers <Ui id="vehicles-install-sample" /> and <Ui id="vehicles-tab-game" />.

## See what an update changes

When a vehicle has several versions installed, its details show an update summary of the newest one, compared with the version before it:

- the changes the package's author wrote, when there are some;
- what TruckPaint finds by comparing the two versions: textures whose layout changed, textures resized, and textures added or removed. With nothing to show, it says <Ui id="vehicles-no-changes" />.

Under it, <Ui id="vehicles-projects" /> lists your recent projects that use an older version, and the open project, with the work the update asks for: "Blue Wave Biarritz: 1 texture, 15 objects to reposition". TruckPaint can't tell which areas of a template moved, so every object on a texture whose layout changed is counted. The projects are read without being opened or changed. Only recent projects are listed, as the list says.

<Shot name="vehicles/update-summary" alt="The Vehicle Library showing the sample truck’s update summary: the layout changed on Standard cab, Side skirts added, and the project Blue Wave Biarritz with the objects to reposition." />

<Ui id="vehicles-later" /> closes the library. <Ui id="update-apply" /> runs <Ui id="cmd-update-template" /> on the open project when it uses that vehicle; otherwise it is disabled, and the other projects are updated when you open them.

## Update a project's vehicle

When the status of a vehicle reads <Ui id="vehicles-status-update" />, its details offer <Ui id="cmd-update-template" />. It closes the library and moves that vehicle of the open project to the newer version: the templates are replaced and your artwork is kept. Textures whose layout changed are marked to check. You can also start it from the vehicle's card in the [Project space](../../fleet/project/).

## Share or remove a version

Next to each installed version:

- <Ui id="vehicles-export" /> saves that version as a `.tpv` file, to give to a friend or keep as a backup. Templates taken from the base games belong to SCS Software: check that you may share them.
- <Ui id="vehicles-remove" /> deletes that version from the library, after a confirmation. Your projects are never changed: each project keeps a copy of its templates.

A custom vehicle also offers <Ui id="vehicles-new-version" />, to make its next version after a game update: see [Custom vehicles](../custom-vehicles/).
