Each texture has its own stack of objects. The <Ui id="cmd-tab-layers" /> tab of the left panel (key 2, or <Ui id="menu-view" /> › <Ui id="cmd-tab-layers" />) shows the stack of the active texture as a tree, topmost object first. Switch texture and the tab follows.

<Shot name="workshop/layers" alt="The Layers tab of the curtain body, with one group per zone (Side (left), Side (right), Rear doors, Front bulkhead and Roof) and Side (left) selected." />

The header names the texture and counts its top-level objects. Each row shows the object's kind icon, its name, an eye toggle and a lock toggle. A text without a name of its own shows its first line, so you can tell your lettering apart.

## Select from the list

- Click a row to select its object, even inside a group.
- Ctrl-click (⌘-click on macOS) adds or removes a row.
- Shift-click selects every row between the last clicked one and this one.

It works both ways: selecting on the canvas highlights the rows, and opens the groups that hold them.

## Change the order

Objects higher in the list are drawn on top. To move them:

- drag rows up or down; a line shows where they will land. Several selected rows move together;
- drop rows onto the middle of a group row to put them inside that group, at its top;
- or use <Ui id="menu-object" /> › <Ui id="cmd-bring-forward" /> (Alt+Ctrl+]) and <Ui id="cmd-send-backward" /> (Alt+Ctrl+[).

## Group objects

Groups keep the parts of a design together: the stripes of a band, the lettering of a door. Clicking a grouped object on the canvas selects the whole group, so it moves as one (Ctrl-click reaches the object inside).

| Command | Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| <Ui id="cmd-group" /> | Ctrl+G | wraps the selected objects in a new group |
| <Ui id="cmd-ungroup" /> | Ctrl+Shift+G | puts the group's objects back in its place |
| <Ui id="cmd-new-layer" /> | Ctrl+Shift+N | adds an empty group at the top, named "Layer 1", "Layer 2"… |
| <Ui id="cmd-duplicate-layer" /> | | duplicates the selected rows |
| <Ui id="cmd-delete-layer" /> | | deletes the selected rows |

Group and Ungroup are in the <Ui id="menu-object" /> menu, the layer commands in the <Ui id="menu-layer" /> menu. All of them are also in the tab's right-click menu and its footer buttons, and the + button of the header adds a layer.

New shapes go at the top of the active layer: the selected group, or the group holding the selection. Select a layer before drawing to draw into it.

Collapse a group row to keep a long list short; selecting one of its objects on the canvas opens it again.

## Hide and lock

- The **eye** hides an object, or a group and everything in it. A hidden object stays in the list but isn't drawn on the canvas, can't be clicked, and **isn't exported**. A texture whose objects are all hidden counts as empty, and is exported with the game's color.
- The **lock** keeps an object from being selected or changed on the canvas, which is handy for a background you keep clicking by mistake. Locked objects can still be selected in the list, to unlock them.

Hiding or locking a selected object deselects it.

## Objects to reposition

After an update changes a texture's layout, every object that was on it is marked with a warning sign in the list: hover the sign to see why. A group shows the mark when an object inside it is marked. Move, resize or rotate an object to clear its mark; changing its colors, text or name keeps it. <Ui id="texture-mark-checked" /> clears every mark of the texture, and Undo brings marks back.

<Shot name="workshop/layers-to-reposition" alt="The Layers tab after an update: each object of the redrawn texture is marked with a warning sign." />

## Rename

Double-click a name, or right-click the row and choose <Ui id="undo-rename" />. Type the new name and press Enter; Escape cancels. An empty name goes back to the default one, such as "Rectangle" or "Group".

Clear names pay off when you come back to a livery months later.

:::note
The template is not a layer: it never appears in this list, can't be selected, and is never exported. Show or hide it with G (see [The Workshop](../overview/#the-template-overlay)).
:::

Reordering, grouping, hiding, locking and renaming can all be undone, one step each.
